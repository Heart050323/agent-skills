# Expressive visual design preset

Use when the user requests a cinematic, layered, or editorial visual direction for a marketing site or portfolio. Do not apply this preset to every interface or override a requested minimal style.

Choose a direction from the brief and assets rather than random variation:

- **Editorial luxury:** warm paper-like surfaces, a contrasting heading face, strong photography, and quiet spacing.
- **Layered technology:** dark or light tonal layers, selective translucency, and clear foreground/background hierarchy.
- **Soft structural:** open spacing, strong type, simple geometry, and restrained ambient shadows.

These are examples, not requirements. Reuse a successful layout when it suits the product; there is no requirement to invent a new aesthetic every run. Preserve the brand's fonts, icons, colors, and existing controls unless the redesign includes them. Check font availability and licensing rather than assuming premium assets exist.

Use asymmetry, split layouts, or layered cards only when they improve the story. At narrow widths, simplify overlapping arrangements and keep reading order, touch targets, and content intact. Rounded CTAs, double borders, floating navigation, blur, and grain are optional.

Animate only where motion adds feedback or clarifies a transition. Keep primary content available immediately, support reduced motion, and test keyboard and touch behavior. Reserve expensive effects for places where actual rendering performance supports them; no compulsory blur reveal on every element.

Use verified content and real assets, or clearly marked demo material. Validate hierarchy, responsive layouts, contrast, focus, and working actions. Report what was rendered and tested; agency price claims are not a measure of quality.
